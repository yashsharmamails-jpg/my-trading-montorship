## PART 3: THE SYSTEM

### Final Simplified System (6 Steps):

```
DIRECTION → LOCATION → SWEEP → BOS → RETEST → ENTER
```

1. **DIRECTION** — What's the H1/H4 bias? Only trade that direction.
2. **LOCATION** — Is price at a significant zone? Only trade at key levels.
3. **SWEEP** — Did price take out stops at that zone? (The trap)
4. **BOS** — Did structure break in my direction after the sweep? (Confirmation)
5. **RETEST** — Is price coming back to the broken level? (Optimal entry)
6. **ENTER** — SL beyond the sweep, TP at next zone, minimum 1:2 R:R.

### Risk Parameters:
- Risk per trade: 0.5%
- Max trades per day: 2
- Max daily loss: 1%
- Max weekly loss: 2.5%
- Target R:R: 1:2 minimum
- Account: $3,000

### Identity Statement:
> *"I am a confirmation-based reversal trader. I trade AFTER smart money traps retail traders and structure confirms my direction. I never anticipate. I react."*

## PART 5: KEY LESSONS LEARNED

1. **No BOS = No Trade** — The #1 rule that separates winning trades from losing ones
2. **FOMO is the killer** — If you feel you MUST enter now, that's the signal to do nothing
3. **Confusion = No trade** — If you can't read the structure clearly, walk away
4. **Predicting ≠ Trading** — "It will definitely do X" is gambling. Wait for confirmation.
5. **Simple > Complex** — 6 steps, not 15. More rules = more confusion = more rule-breaking
6. **Saturday = OFF** — The routine protects you from yourself
7. **Counter-trend is advanced** — For Phase 3+, not Phase 1. Stick to trading WITH the BOS direction.
