## KEY PSYCHOLOGICAL DISCOVERY

### Pattern: "Mid-Trade Conviction Loss"

**What happens:**
1. Student enters with 100% clarity (all steps checked, logic clear)
2. Trade goes into normal drawdown
3. Brain immediately starts questioning the trade
4. Student says "I can't find the logic anymore"
5. Wants to exit at breakeven or take tiny profit
6. Logic hasn't changed — only PERCEPTION has changed due to FEAR

**Root cause:** Fear of loss overrides rational analysis. Amygdala hijack.

**The fix (assigned):**
- Read Trading in the Zone (particularly 5 Fundamental Truths)
- Accept: "My SL is my invalidation. Until it's hit, the trade is valid."
- New rule: Do NOT open trade management for 30 minutes after entry
- Repeat: "I am executing my edge. This is one trade in a series of 50."
