> **Run through this BEFORE every single entry.**
> **If any answer is "NO" — DO NOT TRADE.**

| # | Question | ✅ / ❌ |
|---|---|---|

> ⚠️ **If ANY Rule is ❌ → NO TRADE. Walk away.**

## 🔍 FINAL GUT CHECK

### Question 1:
> *"Am I entering because my system says to, or because I FEEL like it?"*

| Answer | Action |
|---|---|
| System says to | ✅ Proceed |
| I feel like it | ❌ Stop |

### Question 2:
> *"If this trade loses, will I be at peace because I followed my rules?"*

| Answer | Action |
|---|---|
| Yes | ✅ Proceed |
| No | ❌ Something is wrong — review again |

### Question 3:
> *"Am I chasing this entry or is price coming to me?"*

| Answer | Action |
|---|---|
| Coming to me | ✅ Proceed |
| Chasing | ❌ You missed it — move on |

---

## 📌 AFTER ENTRY

- [ ] Set SL and TP on the platform (actual orders, not mental)
- [ ] Take a screenshot of the entry
- [ ] Log it in your journal
- [ ] Step away from the screen (set alert for SL/TP)
- [ ] Do NOT modify the trade unless moving SL to BE after 1R

---

## 🚨 THE ANTI-FOMO REMINDER

> 💡 *"If I feel like I MUST enter RIGHT NOW or I'll miss it — that is my signal to do NOTHING."*

> 💡 *"The best trades feel calm. If I feel urgency, my lizard brain is gambling."*

> 💡 *"Missing a trade = $0 cost. Breaking rules = real money lost + confidence destroyed."*

## 🌳 QUICK DECISION TREE

```
Is HTF bias clear?
│
├── ❌ NO → No trade. Done.
│
└── ✅ YES → Is there a key zone nearby?
    │
    ├── ❌ NO → No trade. Done.
    │
    └── ✅ YES → Did price sweep liquidity at that zone?
        │
        ├── ❌ NO → WAIT. Don't anticipate.
        │
        └── ✅ YES → Did price give BOS in my direction?
            │
            ├── ❌ NO → WAIT. Don't anticipate.
            │
            └── ✅ YES → Is price retesting the broken structure?
                │
                ├── ❌ NO → WAIT for retest. Don't chase.
                │
                └── ✅ YES → Is R:R minimum 1:2?
                    │
                    ├── ❌ NO → Skip. Not worth the risk.
                    │
                    └── ✅ YES → ✅ EXECUTE THE TRADE
```
