## 📌 JOURNAL RULES

1. **Journal EVERY trade** — especially the ones you're embarrassed about.
2. **Be honest** — lying in your journal is lying to yourself.
3. **Grade based on PROCESS, not outcome** — A losing trade with perfect execution is an A.
4. **Review weekly** — don't just log and forget.
5. **After 30 trades** — you'll have enough data to see your real patterns.
