## ✅ THE 7 RULES — Entry Checklist (ALL Must Be True)

---

### Rule 1: HTF Bias Identified

| Check | Details |
|---|---|
| What to do | Determine H4/H1 structure — bullish or bearish |
| Bullish | Higher highs + higher lows |
| Bearish | Lower highs + lower lows |
| If unclear/choppy | **NO TRADE** |

---

### Rule 2: Key Zone Identified

| Check | Details |
|---|---|
| What to do | Mark next significant S/R, supply/demand, or liquidity pool |
| Zone must be | Clear and untested (or freshly created) |
| Skip if | Tested 3+ times already (zone is weak) |

---


### Rule 3: Liquidity Sweep

| Check | Details |
|---|---|
| What to look for | Price takes out obvious highs/lows (stop hunts) |
| Signs | Wicks through key levels, equal highs/lows swept |
| Why it matters | Smart money grabbing orders before reversing |
| **No sweep** | **= No trade. The trap must happen first.** |

---

### Rule 4: Break of Structure (BOS)

| Check | Details |
|---|---|
| What to look for | After the sweep, price breaks significant short-term structure |
| For shorts | Price breaks below a recent higher low |
| For longs | Price breaks above a recent lower high |
| **No BOS** | **= You are GUESSING. No trade.** |
| Timeframe | BOS on M5/M15 is sufficient |

---

### Rule 5: Entry on Retest

| Check | Details |
|---|---|
| After BOS, wait for | Retest of broken structure OR order block OR 50% retracement |
| **Do NOT chase** | Let price come to you |
| If no retest | Missed trade = $0 cost (better than a loss) |

---

### Rule 6: Stop Loss Placement

| Direction | SL Placement |
|---|---|
| Shorts | Above the liquidity sweep high + 2-3 pip buffer |
| Longs | Below the liquidity sweep low + 2-3 pip buffer |
| **Never** | Move SL against your position |
| If SL too wide | Skip the trade (can't get 1:2 R:R) |

---

### Rule 7: Take Profit

| Check | Details |
|---|---|
| TP location | Next significant demand/supply zone on H1/H4 |
| Minimum R:R | 1:2 required before entering |
| If zone doesn't give 1:2 | Skip the trade |
| Execution | Close full position at TP. No hoping for more. |
