## THE 7 RULES — Entry Checklist (ALL Must Be True):

### Rule 1: HTF Bias Identified
- On H4/H1, determine if market is bullish or bearish
- Look for: Higher highs/lows (bullish) or Lower highs/lows (bearish)
- **Only trade in the direction of HTF bias**
- If structure is unclear/choppy = NO TRADE

### Rule 2: Key Zone Identified
- Mark the next significant S/R level, supply/demand zone, or liquidity pool
- This is WHERE you expect a reaction
- Zone must be clear and untested (or freshly created)
- Old zones that have been tested 3+ times = weak, skip them

### Rule 3: Liquidity Sweep
- Price must take out obvious highs or lows (stop hunts)
- Look for: Wicks through key levels, equal highs/lows swept
- This is smart money grabbing orders before reversing
- **No sweep = No trade. The trap must happen first.**

### Rule 4: Break of Structure (BOS) in Your Direction
- After the sweep, price must break a significant short-term structure level
- For shorts: Price must break below a recent higher low
- For longs: Price must break above a recent lower high
- **This is your confirmation. Without this, you are GUESSING.**
- BOS on M5/M15 is sufficient

### Rule 5: Entry on Retest
- After BOS, wait for price to retest:
  - The broken structure level, OR
  - The order block that caused the BOS, OR
  - A 50% retracement of the impulse move
- **Do NOT chase. Let price come to you.**
- If it doesn't retest = missed trade = $0 cost (better than a loss)

### Rule 6: Stop Loss Placement
- **For shorts:** SL above the liquidity sweep high + small buffer (2-3 pips on Forex, $1-2 on Gold)
- **For longs:** SL below the liquidity sweep low + small buffer
- Never move SL against your position
- If SL is too wide for 1:2 R:R = skip the trade

### Rule 7: Take Profit at Next HTF Zone
- TP must be at the next significant demand/supply zone on H1/H4
- Minimum 1:2 R:R required before entering
- If TP zone doesn't give 1:2 = skip the trade
- **Close full position at TP. No moving targets. No hoping for more.**

## TRADE MANAGEMENT:

### During the Trade:
- Set entry, SL, and TP — then WALK AWAY
- Do not stare at the screen
- Do not move SL to breakeven too early (let the trade breathe)
- Move SL to breakeven ONLY after price moves 1R in your favor
- Close at TP. Done.

### If Trade Goes Against You:
- Do nothing. Your SL is there for a reason.
- If you feel the urge to close early = minimize the chart
- A loss is a COST OF DOING BUSINESS. It's not a mistake if you followed rules.
