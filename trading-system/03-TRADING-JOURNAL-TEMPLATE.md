# TRADING JOURNAL

## Why Journal?
> Without data, you're guessing. With data, you're improving.
> Your journal will tell you EXACTLY where you're leaking money and where your edge is.

## IMPORTANT NOTES:

1. **Journal EVERY trade** — even the ones you're embarrassed about. Especially those.
2. **Be honest** — lying in your journal is lying to yourself.
3. **Grade based on PROCESS, not outcome** — A losing trade with perfect execution is an A. A winning trade that broke rules is a D.
4. **Review weekly** — don't just log trades and forget them.
5. **After 30 trades** — you'll have enough data to see your real patterns.

---

*Your journal is your mirror. It shows you who you really are as a trader — not who you think you are.*
