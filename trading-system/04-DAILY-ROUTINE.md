# DAILY TRADING ROUTINE

## The Professional's Schedule
> Funded traders and prop firm traders don't just "open charts and trade." They follow a structured routine that keeps them sharp, disciplined, and emotionally stable.

---

## MORNING ROUTINE (Before Markets — 10:00 AM IST)

**Time: 30 minutes**

### 1. Mindset Reset (5 minutes)
- Read your Identity Statement out loud:
  > "I am a confirmation-based reversal trader. I trade AFTER smart money traps retail traders and structure confirms my direction. I never anticipate. I react."
- Remind yourself: "Today I execute my system. The outcome doesn't matter. My process does."
- Accept that today might be a zero-trade day. That's okay.

### 2. Review Your Rules (5 minutes)
- Glance at your 7-rule checklist
- Remind yourself of your loss limits:
  - Max 2 trades today
  - Max 1% daily loss
  - If I hit my limit, I'm done

### 3. Higher Timeframe Analysis (20 minutes)
- Open H4 and H1 charts for your pairs (XAUUSD, NAS100, EURUSD)
- For each pair, answer:
  - What is the current structure? (Bullish / Bearish / Unclear)
  - Where are the key zones? (Mark them)
  - Where is liquidity sitting? (Equal highs/lows, obvious stop levels)
  - Is there a potential setup forming today?
- Write down your bias for each pair:
  ```
  XAUUSD: _______ (Bullish/Bearish/No bias)
  NAS100: _______ (Bullish/Bearish/No bias)  
  EURUSD: _______ (Bullish/Bearish/No bias)
  ```
- If no clear bias on any pair → that pair is off-limits today

## LONDON SESSION (12:30 PM - 5:30 PM IST)

### Pre-Session (12:30 PM - 1:00 PM)
- Check economic calendar — any high-impact news today?
- If major news within session: wait for it to pass, then look for setups
- Review your HTF markings — are any zones being approached?
- Set alerts on key levels (don't stare at charts waiting)

### Active Trading Window (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM)
- This is when London manipulation typically happens
- Watch for:
  - Liquidity sweeps of Asian session highs/lows
  - Fakeouts above/below overnight ranges
  - BOS after sweeps
- If setup forms → run through Pre-Trade Checklist
- If no setup → do NOT force one. Step away.

### Late London (4:00 PM - 5:30 PM)
- If you haven't traded yet, the best London setups are likely gone
- Begin transitioning to NY preparation
- Do NOT enter "just because I haven't traded yet" — that's boredom, not strategy

---

## NEW YORK SESSION (6:30 PM - 11:30 PM IST)

### Pre-Session (6:30 PM - 7:00 PM)
- How did London go? Any open positions?
- Review structure changes from London session
- Update key zones if needed
- Check for NY session news events

### Active Trading Window (7:00 PM - 10:00 PM)
- NY open often creates manipulation (especially 7:00-8:30 PM IST)
- Watch for:
  - Sweeps of London session highs/lows
  - Failed breakouts of key levels
  - BOS confirmations after sweeps
- If setup forms → run through Pre-Trade Checklist
- Maximum 2 trades total for the day (including London)

### Session Close (10:00 PM - 11:30 PM)
- After 10 PM IST, avoid new entries (liquidity thins out)
- Manage any open trades
- Do NOT start new positions this late

---

## POST-SESSION ROUTINE (After Trading — 15 minutes)

### 1. Journal Your Trades (10 minutes)
- Fill out the journal template for EVERY trade taken today
- Include screenshots
- Grade each trade (A/B/C/D)
- Be honest about emotions

### 2. Daily Debrief (5 minutes)
Answer these questions:
```
Did I follow my system today? YES / NO
How many trades did I take? ___
How many were "A" grade? ___
Did I feel any FOMO, revenge, or emotional urges? YES / NO
If yes, did I act on them? YES / NO
One thing I did well today: _______________
One thing to improve tomorrow: _______________
```

### 3. Shutdown
- Close all charts
- Close TradingView
- Close your trading platform
- **You are done for the day. No peeking at charts at midnight.**
- Go do something non-trading related (exercise, family, hobbies)

---

## WEEKLY ROUTINE:

### Sunday Evening (30-45 minutes):
1. Complete Weekly Review in your journal
2. Review your System Rules document (re-read it)
3. Mark key HTF levels for the upcoming week
4. Check economic calendar for the week
5. Identify which days have high-impact news (plan around them)
6. Set your intention: "This week I will focus on _______________"

### Wednesday Mid-Week Check (10 minutes):
- How is the week going?
- Am I following my rules?
- Am I within my risk limits?
- Do I need to adjust anything?
- If I've hit my weekly loss limit (2.5%) → no more trading this week

## RULES FOR SCREEN TIME:

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| No charts before morning routine | Don't let price action influence your mindset before analysis |
| No trading on phone | Phone = impulse trades = broken rules |
| Set alerts, don't stare | Staring creates FOMO and premature entries |
| Max 4 hours of active chart time per day | More than this = overtrading brain |
| No charts after shutdown routine | You need mental recovery time |

---

## IF YOU HAVE A LOSING DAY:

1. Journal the trades honestly
2. Ask: "Did I follow my rules?"
   - **YES:** It's a normal cost of business. Your system is still intact. Move on.
   - **NO:** Write down WHY you broke rules. What emotion caused it? What triggered you?
3. Do NOT look at charts again today
4. Exercise, walk, or do something physical (burns off cortisol)
5. Come back tomorrow fresh with the mindset: "New day, same system."

---

## IF YOU HAVE A WINNING DAY:

1. Journal the trades
2. Do NOT celebrate by "trading more because I'm hot"
3. Do NOT increase lot size tomorrow because you feel confident
4. Stick to the routine. Consistency is the goal, not big days.
5. Acknowledge: "I followed my process and the market rewarded me. Good."

---

## THE GOLDEN RULES OF ROUTINE:

1. **Same process, every day, regardless of yesterday's results.**
2. **The routine protects you from yourself.**
3. **Skipping the routine = you're not a professional. You're a gambler with a chart open.**
4. **Funded traders follow routines. Blown accounts come from chaos.**
