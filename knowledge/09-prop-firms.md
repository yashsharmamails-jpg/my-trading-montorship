## 🎯 The Core Truth

> **Industry pass rate: 5-10%. Less than 7% of traders survive long enough to get a payout.**
>
> The prop firm model is built to extract evaluation fees from failures. You are NOT the customer — you're the product.
>
> This is winnable, but only with extreme discipline. Same patterns that destroy retail accounts (Day 2 spirals) destroy prop accounts faster.

## 1. Why Prop Firms Exist

### The Business Model

- Charge $100-500+ for evaluation fee
- Trader has 30-60 days to hit profit target while staying within drawdown rules
- ~90% of traders fail evaluation → firm keeps the fee
- ~10% pass → trade with firm's "capital" (often demo with payout from new evaluation fees)
- Of the 10%, only ~70% reach payout (= 7% of all entrants)

### Implication

**You're competing against the math, not against the firm.** They're not trying to fail you — most traders fail themselves with bad psychology.

## 3. Drawdown Types — Critical Distinction

### Static Drawdown
- Anchored to **initial starting balance**
- Provides expanding safety as account grows
- Example: $50K start, $5K max DD = $45K floor (forever)
- **Easier psychologically** — you always know your true floor

### Trailing Drawdown (Intraday)
- Tracks **peak unrealized equity** in real-time
- If you float +$1,000 and close at +$200, threshold permanently moves up by $1,000
- **Punishes swing traders, rewards fast scalpers**
- Hardest to manage psychologically

### Trailing Drawdown (End-Of-Day)
- Tracks **peak closed equity** at end of each day
- More forgiving than intraday trailing
- Punishes giveback days but not floating P&L

### Implication For Your System

Your 6-step framework typically holds trades 20 min - 2 hours. Either static or EOD trailing works. Avoid intraday trailing if possible (you'd need to take partials, which degrades expectancy).

## 4. Common Challenge Rules

### Profit Target
- Phase 1: 8-10% in 30 days
- Phase 2 (verification): 5% in 60 days
- Funded: ongoing, no time limit

### Daily Drawdown
- Typically 4-5% of starting balance
- HARD STOP — exceeded = instant fail
- This is MUCH harder than your personal 1% daily limit

### Max Drawdown
- Typically 10-12% of starting balance
- Account-killer
- Your 0.5% risk × 24 trades = ~12% theoretical max — barely fits

### Min Trading Days
- Often 5-10 days minimum
- Prevents lottery-ticket strategies
- Forces consistency

### Consistency Rules (Apex Specific)
- 30% rule: no single day's profit can exceed 30% of total profit
- Forces steady gains, prevents "lucky day" passes

## 5. Strategy Adaptation For Prop Accounts

### Your Current Personal Account: $3,000, 0.5% risk

### Prop Challenge Account: Typically $50K - $100K (demo)

### Adaptations Required

#### Reduce Risk Per Trade

- Personal: 0.5% × $3,000 = $15 per trade
- Prop: 0.25% - 0.5% × $50,000 = $125 - $250 per trade
- **But account drawdown is tight** — likely use 0.25% for first half of challenge
- After 5%+ buffer built, can increase to 0.5%

#### Tighter Daily Loss Management

- Personal rule: 1% daily max loss
- Prop rule: 4-5% daily max loss
- **But your psychology breaks at 1% already** — keep your 1% rule, ignore prop's higher allowance

#### No Counter-Trend Trades

- Personal: technically allowed (you've taken some)
- Prop: NEVER. The math doesn't allow drawdown for "exploration"
- Stay disciplined to HTF direction only

#### Avoid News Days Entirely

- Personal: discretionary
- Prop: skip every high-impact news day
- The risk-reward of trading news in a challenge is awful

## 6. The Psychology Of "Someone Else's Money"

### The Phenomenon

Trading prop money feels different than personal money. You'd think it'd be EASIER (no personal capital at risk), but it's typically HARDER:

- You feel pressure to perform
- Failure feels shameful
- $200 evaluation fee feels like wasted money on a fail
- The "free" money psychology breaks (it's actually costing you the evaluation fee)

### Common Failure Modes

1. **Overtrading early** — chasing the profit target in week 1
2. **Doubling down after early losses** — protecting the evaluation fee investment
3. **Conservative early, reckless late** — running out of time
4. **Risk-violation in last week** — desperation move

### Solution

**Treat prop account as if it's your $3K account scaled up.** Same rules. Same patience. Same risk %. Same daily limits.

If you can't pass with your normal trading, you can't trade. The evaluation is testing whether you can be a real trader.

## 7. The Pass Rate Reality

### Why 90%+ Fail

Industry data + research shows:
- ~50% fail due to risk violations (revenge sizing, daily DD exceeded)
- ~30% fail due to time pressure (couldn't hit profit target)
- ~20% fail due to actually losing money

### What Survivors Do Differently

1. **Take only HIGH-quality setups** (skip B-grade entirely)
2. **Stop trading after hitting -2% in a single day** (well below the 4-5% rule)
3. **Skip news days entirely**
4. **Use 0.25% risk per trade** for first 50% of challenge
5. **Refuse to revenge trade** — accept losing days as cost of business
6. **Trade small lot sizes** even with bigger account
7. **Walk away** when daily target is hit (don't push)

## 8. The Day-Math Of A Challenge

### Apex 50K Account Example

- Profit target: 8% = $4,000
- Max DD: $2,500 (5%)
- Daily DD: $1,500 (3%)

### Strategy
- Risk 0.25% = $125 per trade
- Target 1:2 R:R = $250 win
- Win rate: 50% target
- Expectancy: ~$62 per trade

### Pace
- $4,000 / $62 = 64 trades to hit target
- 64 trades / 30 days = 2.1 trades/day
- **Right at your normal 2-trade limit. No need to push.**

### Drawdown Buffer
- $2,500 max DD / $125 per loss = 20 consecutive losses possible
- At 50% WR, probability of 20 consecutive losses ≈ 0.0001%
- **Math says you can pass IF you stay disciplined.**

## 9. Don't Take A Challenge Until...

### Prerequisites

You should NOT attempt a funded challenge until ALL of these are true:

- [ ] 50+ live personal trades with > 1.5 profit factor
- [ ] 80%+ rules compliance over last 30 trades
- [ ] Zero manual exits in last 30 trades
- [ ] Zero overtrading days in last 60 days
- [ ] Mental health stable for 6+ months (no medication changes, no episodes)
- [ ] Sleep regular (7+ hours/night for 30+ days)
- [ ] Freelancing income stable (not depending on trading payout)

### Without These

Taking a challenge is gambling. You'll fail, lose the fee, and damage your trading psychology further.

**This is a Month 5-6 decision (or later). Don't even think about it now.**
