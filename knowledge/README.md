## 🗂️ How This Folder Works

This is **NOT another system to follow.** It's a reference library you consult when:
- A pattern shows up and you want to understand the science behind it
- You need a specific intervention for a specific psychological issue
- You're refining your technical approach (zones, structure, regimes)
- You're preparing for backtesting or a funded challenge
- The mentor needs to anchor advice in research rather than opinion

## ⚠️ Important Caveats

1. **This is reference material, not a curriculum.** Don't try to read everything in 2 days. Use it when you need it.
2. **The science doesn't replace the system.** Your 6-step framework + journal + 0.5% risk are still the operational rules.
3. **Where research conflicts with your system, the system wins for now.** Phase 1 is about consistency, not optimization. Refinements come in Phase 3.
4. **Mental health context (your psychosis recovery) modifies general advice.** Where general traders can push, you must protect.
5. **All citations are in source-docs.** If a claim feels controversial, check the source.
