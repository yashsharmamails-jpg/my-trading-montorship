## 📚 Citation Style

The deep-dive document uses inline citations with numbers (e.g., "research shows ¹⁹"). All citation references are in the "Works Cited" section at the end of `deep-dive-full.md`.

Most cited research is from:
- Peer-reviewed psychology journals (NIH, PMC, Frontiers in Psychiatry)
- Academic books (Kahneman, Tversky, Festinger, Skinner, etc.)
- Trading-specific research (FXNX, QuantVPS, ACY Securities)
- Industry data (CBOE GVZ, SPDR, J.P. Morgan, PIMCO)
