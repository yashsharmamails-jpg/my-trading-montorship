# 🚨 Intervention Quick Reference

> **Use case:** You're in or just exited a pattern. You need the SPECIFIC fix in 60 seconds. No theory, just action.

---

## 🆘 The Lookup Table

When you catch yourself in a pattern, find the row → execute the intervention.

| Pattern | The Tell | The Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| **Bias Lock** | Took 2+ same-direction trades, all losing | INVERT chart Y-axis. Force opposite-side analysis. Stop trading direction for 24 hours. |
| **Loss-Day Aversion** | "I can't close red today" | Mask P&L display, switch to R-multiples only. Accept any outcome. |
| **Catastrophizing** | A small loss feels huge | Calculate actual $ loss as % of account. Write the math on paper before any next action. 60-min cooldown. |
| **Revenge Sizing** | Tightening SL to fit more lots | STOP. Position size is calculated FROM SL, never the reverse. Cancel the order. |
| **Shame Lying** | Want to omit a trade from journal | Send full MT4 history to mentor BEFORE writing anything else. |
| **Compulsive Continuation** | "One more trade" after 2 done | Power down workstation physically. Leave the room. 24-hour lockout. |
| **Mid-Trade Conviction Loss** | "I can't find the logic anymore" | Minimize chart. Walk away. Trade thesis was set BEFORE entry. Don't re-evaluate. |
| **Chart Addiction** | Demo trading, multiple windows, late-night peeks | Cold Turkey blocker on. Phone in another room. Physical environment shift. |
| **Anticipation Over Reaction** | "I think it WILL do X" | Rewrite analysis as "IF X happens, THEN Y is valid." No predictions. |

## 📞 The Crisis Protocols

### Crisis Level 1: Single Pattern Detected
**Action:** Use intervention from table above. Pause 30 minutes. Resume if calm.

### Crisis Level 2: Multiple Patterns In One Session
**Action:** Stop trading for the day. Journal what happened. No charts for 4 hours.

### Crisis Level 3: Same Pattern 3 Days In A Row
**Action:** Stop trading for 1 week. Mentor session. Identify root behavioral cause.

### Crisis Level 4: Spiral Like May 25 (5 trades, broke daily limit)
**Action:** 2-day pause minimum. Full review. Mental health check.

### Crisis Level 5: Lying To Mentor / Hiding Trades
**Action:** Tell the truth NOW. Then pause minimum 1 week. Without honesty, mentorship is dead.

## 🛡️ The Pre-Trade Mental Filter

Before EVERY entry, run this 30-second check:

1. **Direction:** Is H4 aligned? (NOT just M5/M1)
2. **Setup:** All 6 boxes ticked or am I forcing?
3. **Time:** Within killzone?
4. **State:** Am I calm or am I trying to "fix" something?
5. **Sleep:** Did I get 7+ hours last night?
6. **Trade #:** Is this trade #1 or #2 today? (If #3+, STOP.)
7. **R:R:** Minimum 1:2 after spread?

**If ANY answer is "no" or hesitant → SKIP THE TRADE.**

---

## 🚦 The In-Trade Mental Filter

After entry, if you feel the urge to "manage" the trade, ask:

1. **Has the structural SL level been violated?** No → don't touch.
2. **Has news appeared that wasn't expected at entry?** No → don't touch.
3. **Has the H4 thesis broken?** No → don't touch.
4. **Are you "managing" or "panicking"?** Be honest.

**If all 4 say "don't touch" → DON'T TOUCH.**

Remember: Mid-trade conviction loss is a documented pattern. The thesis was made before entry. Stick to it.

---

## ✋ The Post-Loss Mental Filter

After ANY losing trade, BEFORE doing anything else:

1. **Walk away from charts for 30 minutes minimum.**
2. **Was the loss within rules?** YES → it's a normal cost of doing business. Move on.
3. **Was the loss because of broken rules?** YES → write the rule break in journal, no more trades today.
4. **Calculate exact loss in $ and as % of account.** Reality-check the size.
5. **Resist the urge to "fix it."** Today's P&L doesn't matter.

---

## 💸 The Profit-Day Mental Filter

After a winning trade (yes, this matters too):

1. **Don't celebrate big.** It's one data point.
2. **Don't take a "free" extra trade.** Profit doesn't earn you the right to break rules.
3. **Journal the win exactly like you'd journal a loss.**
4. **Stop after 2 trades regardless.** Even if you're "hot."

---

## 🌙 The End-Of-Day Mental Filter

Before logging off:

1. **Close all charts.** All. Even "just to check tomorrow's setup."
2. **Journal each trade** with grade (A/B/C/D).
3. **Did I trade my plan or did the plan trade me?**
4. **One thing I did well today. One thing to improve tomorrow.**
5. **Phone away by 11 PM IST.** Sleep is risk management.

## 🆘 Emergency Contact Protocol

### If you find yourself:
- Wanting to increase risk to recover losses
- Considering quitting freelancing for trading
- Feeling manic, depressed, or anxious
- Skipping medication
- Sleeping less than 6 hours
- Lying to mentor or hiding trades

### Do this:
1. **Stop all trading immediately.**
2. **Tell mentor honestly.**
3. **Talk to mom or psychiatrist if mental health is involved.**
4. **No charts for minimum 48 hours.**
5. **Re-entry only after restored stability.**

## 🎯 The 5 Anchors To Re-Read When Tempted

When you're about to break a rule, read these in order:

### Anchor 1: Identity Statement
> *"I am a confirmation-based reversal trader. I trade AFTER smart money traps retail traders. I never anticipate. I react."*

### Anchor 2: The Math
> *"My system: 50% WR × 1:2 R:R × 0.5% risk = +0.25% per trade. 25 trades/month = +6.25% account growth. The math is enough. I don't need more."*

### Anchor 3: The Hierarchy
> *"Mental health > Freelancing > Trading. Order matters. Today doesn't change tomorrow."*

### Anchor 4: The Day 2 Lesson
> *"The +$45 from Trade #7 is a debt, not a win. I'm not allowed to celebrate rule-breaks just because the market saved me."*

### Anchor 5: The Core Question
> *"If today is one of 250 trading days this year, does this single trade actually matter?"*

(Answer: no.)

## 🔁 The Recovery Protocol After A Spiral

Like May 25, when multiple patterns fire:

### Day 1 (Crisis Day)
- Charts off
- Journal honestly
- Sleep 8+ hours

### Day 2 (Pause Day 1)
- No live, no demo
- Read book (Trading in the Zone)
- Backtest 5 setups on replay
- Walk outside

### Day 3 (Pause Day 2)
- Same as Day 2
- Reflect on what triggered the spiral
- Write a "letter to future self" describing what to avoid

### Day 4 (Re-Entry)
- Single trade max
- HTF aligned only
- Walk away after entry
- Close after first trade regardless of outcome
