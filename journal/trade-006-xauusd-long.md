**"Still believe it can move up" — said three times in a row.**

When the market gives you the same answer 3 times, **the market is the answer.** Bias lock keeps me arguing with price action that has already decided.

> *"The market doesn't owe me my thesis. If I have to fight to stay in a direction, I should be out of the trade."*
