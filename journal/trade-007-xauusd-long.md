## 🧠 Self-Assessment

### What was happening in my head:

> *"After 1 win, 1 BE, and 1 loss, I cannot accept closing in net loss. I need to recover or my future as a trader is in question."*

This is **catastrophizing.** The actual situation:
- Morning trade: +$35.60 ✅
- After all 4 NY losses combined: still net positive on the day
- Worst case scenario: -$5 day → still ~99.83% account intact

**There was no actual emergency. The emergency was emotional.**

### Why "forgot the setup"?

Because the brain wasn't trading the chart anymore. It was trading the FEELING (shame from previous losses + fear of finishing red).

When you're trading feelings, you don't need a setup. You just need an entry button.

### Why high quantity + tight stop?

To make MORE money on the bounce. Same 0.5% risk %, but tighter stop means more lots = more dollar reward per pip moved. **This is revenge sizing dressed up as proper risk management.**

### Why manual close?

> *"I had to lock it in. If price reversed and I gave back the +$45, I would have spiraled even harder."*

This is the final tell: I was protecting myself from MYSELF. Knowing I couldn't trust myself to hold means I shouldn't have been in the trade.

## 🎓 Lesson Learned

### THE RESCUE TRADE TRAP:

**When you trade to "save the day," you've already lost the day.**

The day was already +$20.60 BEFORE Trade #7. I didn't need to rescue anything. I needed to STOP.

But the brain doesn't see it that way. The brain saw -$15 + $0 + -$15 = "I'm losing." It didn't count the morning win, because the morning win felt "old."

### The Most Dangerous Lesson:

**Today my brain learned: "Breaking all the rules in one trade can save the day."**

That's the lesson I have to UNLEARN. Because next time the market won't bounce. And the same brain that took Trade #7 today will take a Trade #7 tomorrow that goes -$200.
