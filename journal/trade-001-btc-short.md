## 🎓 Lesson Learned

**The trade went into drawdown, then to +1R, then back to BE.**

This is **normal market behavior.** Not every winning setup gives full TP. Sometimes BE is the natural outcome.

The win wasn't in the P&L. The win was in the **execution**:
- I held through doubt
- I followed the rule (BE only at +1R)
- I didn't break discipline

> *"A BE trade following the rules is a winning trade in development."*

---

## 🔑 Key Insight

**Mid-trade conviction loss identified as personal weakness.**

When in drawdown, my brain said *"I can't find the logic anymore"* — but the logic was unchanged. Fear filtered my perception. This is exactly what Mark Douglas describes in *Trading in the Zone, Chapter 5*.

**Fix:** Trust the SL. It's the only invalidation point. Don't re-analyze a valid trade mid-flight.
