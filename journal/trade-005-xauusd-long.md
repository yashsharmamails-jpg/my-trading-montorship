## 🧠 Self-Assessment

### Why this trade was taken:
After Trade #4's -1R loss, brain said: *"The bullish bias is still right, the entry was just too early. Let me try again."*

This is **bias lock** — refusing to update analysis after market disagrees.

### Was the setup actually valid?
Marginal at best. The first sweep had already happened. The second attempt was either:
- A duplicate setup (over-fitting the same thesis)
- Or a forced entry to recover money

### Did I run the pre-trade checklist?
**No.** Once in spiral mode, the checklist is the first thing to go.

## 🎓 Lesson Learned

**The first revenge trade is the cheapest one.**

This trade was BE — $0 cost. But it confirmed to the brain that "I can keep going." That's the dangerous lesson.

The cheap revenge trade enables the expensive one (Trade #6).

> *"BE on a rule-breaking trade is worse than -1R on a clean trade. Because it teaches the brain that breaking rules is consequence-free."*
