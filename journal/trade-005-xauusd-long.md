- A duplicate setup (over-fitting the same thesis)

## 🎓 Lesson Learned

**The first revenge trade is the cheapest one.**

This trade was BE — $0 cost. But it confirmed to the brain that "I can keep going." That's the dangerous lesson.

The cheap revenge trade enables the expensive one (Trade #6).

> *"BE on a rule-breaking trade is worse than -1R on a clean trade. Because it teaches the brain that breaking rules is consequence-free."*
