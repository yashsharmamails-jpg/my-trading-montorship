## THE SYSTEM WE BUILT:

### Name: Liquidity Sweep + BOS Reversal System

### 6 Steps (Final Simplified Version):

```
DIRECTION → LOCATION → SWEEP → BOS → RETEST → ENTER
```

1. **DIRECTION** — H1/H4 bias. Only trade that direction. If unclear = no trade.
2. **LOCATION** — Price must be at a significant S/R or supply/demand zone.
3. **SWEEP** — Price must take out stops at that zone (the trap). No sweep = no trade.
4. **BOS** — Structure must break in your direction after sweep. No BOS = no trade.
5. **RETEST** — Wait for price to come back to broken level. No chase.
6. **ENTER** — SL beyond the sweep, TP at next zone, minimum 1:2 R:R.

### Risk Parameters:
- Risk per trade: 0.5% ($15 on $3,000)
- Max trades per day: 2
- Max daily loss: 1%
- Max weekly loss: 2.5%
- Target R:R: 1:2 minimum
- Close full position at TP

### Identity Statement:
> "I am a confirmation-based reversal trader. I trade AFTER smart money traps retail traders and structure confirms my direction. I never anticipate. I react."
