## 🎯 The System (Quick Reference)

```
DIRECTION → LOCATION → SWEEP → BOS → RETEST → ENTER
```

1. **DIRECTION** — H1/H4 bias. Only trade that direction.
2. **LOCATION** — Price at a significant S/R or supply/demand zone.
3. **SWEEP** — Stops taken at that zone (the trap).
4. **BOS** — Structure broke in your direction after sweep.
5. **RETEST** — Wait for price to come back to the broken level.
6. **ENTER** — SL beyond sweep, TP at next zone, minimum 1:2 R:R.

## 🔒 Hard Rules

- ❌ No BOS = No Trade
- ❌ No setup = No Trade
- ❌ No manual exits — only SL or TP
- ❌ No trading on Saturdays
- ❌ No new entries after 9 PM IST on Fridays
